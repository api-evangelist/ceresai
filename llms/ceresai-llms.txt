# CeresAI

> CeresAI (formerly Ceres Imaging) is an aerial and satellite agricultural intelligence platform that turns spectral imagery and machine learning into field-level crop-health, yield, water-stress, and risk insights for growers, agribusinesses, lenders, and crop insurers across 40+ crop types and four continents. Backed by Insight Partners.
